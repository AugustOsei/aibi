# AIBI design story

The visitor arrives with one broad question: how can industries use AI today? A five-scene animated guide first shows the complete path from country selection to the adoption gap. The explorer then lets the visitor follow that path directly by choosing a country, an industry and a level of AI integration around an interactive ASCII Earth. The illustrations explain the promise; the globe creates curiosity; the controls turn that curiosity into a clear destination.

The result begins with practical AI uses at the selected level. Visitors can move between standard, integrated and advanced/human-led applications without scrolling through three separate reports. Only after the practical answer does the interface introduce reported adoption and the possible gap. The visual language is calm, precise and non-promotional: an approachable public intelligence tool rather than an AI-generated landing page.
