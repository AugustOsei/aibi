# AIBI interface system

AIBI is a public industry guide, not an AI marketing site or a financial terminal. Its primary experience is one connected question: country → industry → AI integration level. The interface uses a quiet off-white field, deep green ink, lime only for a completed selection, and orange only for geographic markers or editorial emphasis. Typography is restrained enough that the interactive explorer remains above the fold.

An animated five-scene guide precedes the explorer and explains the complete product promise: choose a country, choose an industry, select an AI level, see practical uses, then compare available adoption evidence. Each scene is a bespoke SVG illustration rather than a stock icon, with restrained looping motion and a reduced-motion fallback.

The accurate ASCII Earth is the central exploration object. It can be dragged in two axes, released with momentum, or used to choose a highlighted country; it stays synchronized with accessible controls beside it. Each subsequent choice unlocks only after the previous one. Results show one AI level at a time as clear use-and-outcome rows. Reported adoption and the gap appear afterward as optional evidence, and only when the available data is genuinely comparable. Feedback uses purposeful transitions and unknown evidence is always presented as unavailable—never as zero.
